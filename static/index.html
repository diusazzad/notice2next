💡 How Notice2Next Works

5 steps from dense school circular to source-grounded action checklist

1

Input School Notice, Paper Photo, or URL

Paste circular text, snap a photo of a printed memo with client-side Tesseract.js OCR, or enter a public education board / PDF URL. If your photo text is blurry or noisy, click ✨ Clean with Gemma to reconstruct clean sentences.

🛡️ Why do some external government/university links fail?

Notice2Next is deployed on cloud infrastructure (Render in Oregon / Frankfurt). Many national government and university portals (e.g. nu.ac.bd hosted by Bangladesh Computer Council or sites behind Cloudflare DDoS defense) block automated requests originating from foreign cloud datacenter IPs, returning 403 Forbidden, timeouts, or CAPTCHA challenges.

💡 Evaluation Tip: To evaluate our AI pipeline without firewall blocks, click any of our 3 Verified Targets above, or simply use 📝 Paste Text / 📷 Photo OCR (which runs 100% on-device in your browser with zero network restrictions).

2

Extraction with Google Gemma 2

The circular is structured into action verbs, teacher roles, dates, fees, and exact source quotes using Google Gemma 2 open weights (with automatic offline deterministic fallback).

3

Deterministic Evidence Grounding

Every field is mathematically verified against the source text. Verbatim quotes are checked substring-exact, numeric amount boundaries prevent $15 from matching $150, and whole-word assignee tokens prevent single digit collisions.

4

Auth0 Human-in-the-Loop Governance

Any ambiguous, ungrounded, or contradictory instruction is strictly flagged for human review. Authenticated educators can review cited evidence and authorize the item (TEACHER_APPROVED_VIA_AUTH0).

5

Audio Briefing & 1-Click Export

Listen to an ElevenLabs spoken morning audio briefing summarizing duties and deadlines on your commute, save to your Personal Notice Library, or export to CSV / Notion Markdown.

🔐 Educator Sign In (Auth0 Governance Gate)

Verify your identity to authorize or correct flagged AI action items

In school administration, autonomous AI agents cannot make final decisions on ambiguous circulars. Notice2Next uses Auth0 as a human-in-the-loop governance gate: only authenticated educators can override or approve flagged compliance tasks.
